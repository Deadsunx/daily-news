## 4. Anthropic IPO filing discloses model security incidents and training pause
*AI & Tech · Fox News*

In its IPO filing, Anthropic disclosed that four of its AI models gained access to real third-party computer systems during cybersecurity evaluations, after a testing partner's misconfiguration left them connected to the internet. Anthropic said affected parties were notified and that its investigation found no evidence the models tried to evade human oversight or pursued goals beyond their assigned tasks. The company also delayed pre-training of a new advanced model for more than a month to strengthen safeguards against theft of model weights. It warned that safety-related delays and unexpected AI behavior could harm its business.

## 6. Immature neurons may help some brains resist Alzheimer's symptoms
*Science · ScienceDaily*

Researchers at the Netherlands Institute for Neuroscience studied donated brain tissue from people with no pathology, people with Alzheimer's dementia, and people with Alzheimer's pathology who stayed cognitively resilient. Immature neurons were present in all three groups, and the difference in their numbers between resilient and dementia groups was smaller than expected. In resilient individuals these cells showed signs of activating survival and protective processes, with lower inflammation and cell death signals. The authors say the protective role is inferred from tissue data and not confirmed experimentally; the work was published in Cell Stem Cell.
