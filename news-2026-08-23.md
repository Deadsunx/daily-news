## 3. Nvidia Strikes $6 Billion Licensing Deal With AI Coding Startup Poolside
*AI & Tech · Bloomberg*

Nvidia agreed to pay AI startup Poolside $6 billion to license its "Model Factory" software, which underpins Poolside's Laguna family of open-weight coding models. Nvidia is separately investing $1 billion in Poolside at a $12 billion pre-money valuation and plans to bring on roughly 109 Poolside staff who worked on Laguna. The arrangement is structured as a non-exclusive licensing and investment deal rather than an acquisition, and Poolside's three co-founders will remain in place as the company continues operating independently.

## 4. Hurricane Hunter Data Reveal Early Warning Signs of Strengthening Storms
*Science · University of Miami Rosenstiel School*

Researchers at the University of Miami Rosenstiel School analyzed nearly three decades of NOAA Hurricane Hunter radar data — 1,510 radar analyses from 28 hurricane seasons between 1997 and 2024 — to identify four structural features that precede a tropical cyclone's vertical alignment, a key step before storms can rapidly intensify. The findings could give forecasters additional lead time to flag disorganized storms that are becoming capable of strengthening, aiding evacuation and preparation decisions. The data comes from the TC-RADAR archive of Doppler radar analyses.

## 5. Tooth Count Linked to Nearly Two Extra Years of Survival After Pancreatic Cancer Surgery
*Science · ScienceDaily*

A study of 339 patients who underwent pancreatic cancer surgery at Hiroshima University found that those with at least 21 natural teeth survived a median of 60.7 months after surgery, compared with 39.1 months for patients with 20 or fewer teeth — a gap of nearly two years. Every patient received a pre-operative dental exam as part of the study, published in the Journal of Hepato-Biliary-Pancreatic Sciences. Researchers suspect tooth loss may reflect years of inflammation, frailty, and nutritional decline tied to overall physical resilience.
