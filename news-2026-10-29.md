# Daily News — 2026-10-29

## 1. Israel's election count continues as Likud leads Eisenkot's Yashar party by seven seats
*World & Geopolitics · Reuters*

Turnout in Israel's election was among the highest in the country's history. As of Oct. 28, Likud led Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar party by seven seats, and exit polls projected two Arab parties winning 17 to 18 seats combined, their strongest showing ever. Vote processing is expected to continue until Thursday, after which President Isaac Herzog will select a candidate for prime minister.

## 2. U.S. tells Gulf states to prepare for possible short, intense renewal of Iran war after midterms
*World & Geopolitics · The Wall Street Journal*

The Wall Street Journal reported on Oct. 28 that the United States has told several Middle East countries to prepare for a short, intense military campaign against Iran after the U.S. midterm elections. Two officials said a Patriot air-defense system is being sent from Texas to Israel. The report points to continued risk of renewed conflict in the region.

## 3. Brazil's Flavio Bolsonaro has not conceded; whereabouts unclear after Supreme Court passport order
*World & Geopolitics · Reuters*

Flavio Bolsonaro lost Sunday's Brazilian presidential election and has not conceded, though his party did. Brazil's Supreme Court ordered him to surrender his passports, and police are examining his campaign over alleged foreign funding. Reports conflict on his location: Argentina's Migration Department said he crossed the border and flew to Ecuador, and President Trump said he approves of Bolsonaro coming to the United States.

[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trump-says-he-approves-brazils-flavio-bolsonaro-coming-us-2026-10-28/)

## 4. Federal Reserve holds rates steady with two dissents
*Business & Markets · TheStreet*

The Federal Reserve left interest rates unchanged on Oct. 28, as markets expected; CME FedWatch had priced a hold at about 93%. The statement upgraded its description of the economy to a "strong pace" from "solid" in September. There were two dissents: Cleveland Fed President Hammack and Governor Lisa Cook, whose first dissent since joining the Board in 2022. Chair Warsh was scheduled to hold a press conference at 2:30 PM ET.

## 7. WMO: Atmospheric CO2 reached record 426.8 ppm in 2025
*Science · Reuters*

The World Meteorological Organization reported that atmospheric carbon dioxide averaged 426.8 parts per million in 2025, about 153% of the 1750 pre-industrial level. Reuters reported that further rises are expected with El Nino. The findings are to be presented at the COP31 climate conference in Antalya, Turkey, in November.

[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/cop/greenhouse-gases-hit-record-highs-further-rise-expected-with-el-nio-un-weather-2026-10-27/)
