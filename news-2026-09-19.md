## 2. Congress Approves Sweeping New Sanctions on Russia and Iran
*Business & Markets · RFE/RL*

The U.S. House of Representatives passed the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 by a vote of 262-159, following Senate passage with an 86-11 margin. The legislation targets Russian financial institutions, energy sector, and shadow fleet tankers, while authorizing the president to impose tariffs up to 100 percent on major purchasers of Russian oil and gas. Russia has signaled that signing the bill would make peace negotiations with Ukraine more difficult.

The warning reflects Europe's growing concern about escalating Russian operations beyond traditional military theaters as tensions continue over the Ukraine conflict.
