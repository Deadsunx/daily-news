# Daily News — 2026-08-08

## 1. U.S. Payrolls Fell 23,000 in July as Prior Months Were Revised Down by 103,000

*Business & Markets · U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics*

The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported that nonfarm payroll employment declined by 23,000 in July while the unemployment rate held at 4.1 percent, with losses concentrated in local government education (-50,000) and retail trade (-19,000). Revisions cut May and June payrolls by a combined 103,000, and average hourly earnings rose 3.2 percent over the year. The report reframes the labor market as materially weaker than earlier estimates suggested, and shifts expectations for Federal Reserve policy.

[The Employment Situation — July 2026 (BLS)](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm)

## 2. Ford Names Its Sub-$30,000 Electric Pickup the Fathom, Starting at $28,350

*Automotive · electrive*

Ford revealed that the first production vehicle on its Universal EV Platform is a midsize electric pickup called the Fathom, with a starting price of $28,350 and pre-orders opening in early 2027. Ford says the platform cuts parts per vehicle by 20 percent and assembly workstations by 40 percent, reducing production time by 15 percent. It is the cheapest EV Ford will sell and a test of whether U.S. automakers can build affordable electric trucks profitably.

[Ford names its affordable electric pickup Fathom (electrive)](https://www.electrive.com/2026/08/07/ford-names-its-affordable-electric-pickup-fathom/)

## 3. Tesla and SpaceX Commit $16.8 Billion to a Texas Chip Plant Called Terafab

*AI & Tech · TechCrunch*

Tesla and SpaceX confirmed a semiconductor manufacturing complex named Terafab in Grimes County, Texas, with an initial investment of $16.8 billion. The site is planned to exceed 100 million square feet and will manufacture, package and test logic and memory chips for Tesla vehicles and Optimus robots as well as SpaceX computing systems. The project would move both companies from chip buyers to chip producers, a rare attempt at vertical integration outside the established foundry industry.

[Tesla and SpaceX will invest $16.8B to start building 'Terafab' chip factory in Texas (TechCrunch)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/tesla-and-spacex-will-invest-16-8b-to-start-building-terafab-chip-factory-in-texas/)

## 4. AMD Acquires Taalas, a Startup That Hardwires AI Models Into Silicon

*AI & Tech · CNBC*

AMD agreed to acquire Toronto-based Taalas, founded in 2023, which builds chips that etch individual AI model weights directly into semiconductor metal layers rather than running them on general-purpose accelerators. Financial terms were not disclosed; Taalas had raised about $219 million in venture funding. AMD plans to fold the technology into its accelerator roadmap alongside Instinct GPUs, EPYC processors and ROCm software, signalling that inference cost is becoming the next front in the AI chip contest.

[AMD buys Taalas, startup that hardwires AI models into its silicon (CNBC)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/06/amd-buys-taalas-startup-that-hardwires-ai-models-into-its-silicon.html)

## 5. Uganda's Parliament Approves Sending Troops to the Gaza Stabilization Force

*World & Geopolitics · PBS NewsHour*

Ugandan lawmakers approved a motion from the defence minister to deploy troops to Gaza as part of the International Stabilization Force proposed by the U.S.-led Board of Peace. Uganda joins Morocco, Kazakhstan, Kosovo, Indonesia and Albania in pledging soldiers to the roughly 20,000-strong force, which is to be led by U.S. Maj. Gen. Jasper Jeffers. Opposition lawmakers questioned the government's motives, and the deployment's timing depends on unresolved steps in the Hamas disarmament roadmap.
