## 4. ESA's Juice spacecraft uses Earth flyby to speed toward Jupiter
*Science · ScienceDaily (European Space Agency)*

ESA's Jupiter Icy Moons Explorer passed Earth on September 28, coming within 8,640 km above the Indian Ocean. The gravity assist changed its course by 20 degrees and added 3.5 km/s of speed, needing only one of six planned course corrections. Teams used the pass to test all 10 science instruments. A third and final Earth flyby is planned for January 2029, with arrival at Jupiter in 2031.

[ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261001214102.htm)

## 5. Previously unknown form of metallic iron found in Chang'e-6 lunar soil
*Science · ScienceDaily*

Analysis of lunar soil returned by China's Chang'e-6 mission found a previously unknown form of metallic iron, called gamma-Fe, inside impact-glass particles. According to the report, these particles can preserve stable magnetic properties. The finding adds to understanding of the Moon's magnetic record.

[ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261001214015.htm)

## 6. Enzyme UBE2N identified as possible natural defense against worsening fatty liver disease
*Science · ScienceDaily*

Scientists identified an enzyme, UBE2N, that may act as a natural defense against the worsening of fatty liver disease. The condition is estimated to affect about 100 million Americans. The finding points to a possible target for future treatments, though the report does not describe clinical trials.
