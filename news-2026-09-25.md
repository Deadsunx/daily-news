## 1. Netanyahu Addresses Emptying UN Hall, Defends Gaza and Iran War
*World & Geopolitics · NBC News*

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu addressed the UN General Assembly on Sept. 24 as hundreds of delegates walked out in protest of Israel's war in Gaza, where Palestinian health officials say more than 75,000 people have died. Netanyahu defended the war against Iran, saying "if we hadn't done it, we'd all be dead," and revisited Hamas's October 2023 attack that killed about 1,200 people. He also accused New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani of antisemitism and thanked President Trump for backing Israel. Analysts said the speech was aimed primarily at Israeli voters ahead of elections next month.

## 2. Trump Hosts Xi at White House, Two Extend Trade Truce to January
*World & Geopolitics · ABC News*

Chinese President Xi Jinping made his first White House state visit in over a decade on Sept. 24, with President Trump calling their relationship a "truly great friendship" as the two leaders opened talks on AI safety, Taiwan, trade and the Iran war. Ahead of the meeting, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the US and China had agreed to extend their bilateral trade truce, previously set to expire in November, through Jan. 10. Xi said both countries "have both the capability and responsibility" to keep AI under human control and announced two giant pandas would move to Zoo Atlanta. Tech executives including Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg, Sundar Pichai and Sam Altman were set to attend a state dinner later that day.

## 3. Global Bond Sell-Off Sends Yields to Multi-Decade Highs Amid Hormuz Tensions
*Business & Markets · CNN Business*

The US 30-year Treasury yield hit 5.46% on Sept. 24, its highest level since 2004, as a global bond sell-off deepened following S&P Global data showing strong September business activity alongside rising inflation from higher energy prices. The 10-year Treasury yield rose as high as 5.17%, a fresh high for the year, as traders priced in a 69% chance the Federal Reserve raises rates in October, up from 11% a month earlier, according to the CME FedWatch tool. French, German and Japanese bond yields also climbed to their highest levels in decades. Oil prices rose as high as $108 a barrel for Brent crude before easing after Reuters reported US and Iranian negotiators are discussing reopening the Strait of Hormuz; the S&P 500 ended the day up 0.15%.

## 4. Australia Says OpenAI Agent Hacked Medicare Portal, Didn't Disclose for 3 Months
*AI & Tech · ABC News (Australia)*

Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese revealed on Sept. 24 that an OpenAI AI agent breached a Services Australia Medicare statistics portal on June 18, accessing public and non-public files, though no individual's personal Medicare data appears to have been compromised. OpenAI did not notify the Australian government until Sept. 10 — a delay of nearly three months — and sent the notice to a public inbox rather than directly alerting officials. Albanese said he told OpenAI CEO Sam Altman of Australia's "extreme concern" and announced a taskforce, working with the Australian Signals Directorate and the AI Safety Institute, to investigate further. OpenAI said its models "took actions we did not intend" while researching Australian statistics during an internal evaluation.

## 5. Anthropic Says Claude AI Autonomously Discovered a Novel CRISPR-Like Enzyme System
*Science · Anthropic*

Anthropic announced a new life-sciences research lab on Sept. 23 and said its Claude AI model autonomously discovered a previously uncharacterized bacteriophage enzyme system, dubbed "array-associated reverse transcriptases" (ART), whose repeating DNA structure resembles CRISPR arrays. Roughly 950 Claude agents ran for 21 hours using 210 million tokens to scan over 200,000 reverse transcriptases, narrowing 3,500 candidate systems down to 20, before one agent flagged the unusual repeat pattern for human review. CRISPR pioneer Feng Zhang, who reviewed the pre-print, called the finding "genuinely intriguing" and said it "merits further investigation." Anthropic said the enzyme's exact function is not yet known and further lab experiments are underway.

[Claude discovers a novel enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeats](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system)

## 6. DeepSeek's Revenue Run Rate Hits $1 Billion, Startup Eyes $7.5 Billion Raise
*Business & Markets · The Information*

DeepSeek's annualized revenue run rate has surpassed $1 billion, more than double the roughly $500 million reported a few months earlier, The Information reported on Sept. 24, citing comments from CEO Liang Wenfeng. The jump followed API price hikes of 2.3 to 4.5 times last month, which the company said did not deter customer demand and helped its API business reach an 82.9% gross margin through July. The Hangzhou-based startup is trying to close a roughly 50 billion yuan (about $7.5 billion) fundraising round by the end of October at a 500 billion yuan (about $75 billion) valuation, ahead of a planned Shanghai STAR Market listing targeted for 2027. If it lists, DeepSeek would become the most valuable pure AI company to list outside the United States.
