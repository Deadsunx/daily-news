*World & Geopolitics · Just Security (Early Edition, citing WSJ, CNN and Reuters)*

## 4. Nvidia shares drop nearly 5% as board probes CEO's ties; Nasdaq futures climb
*Business & Markets · TheStreet*

## 5. Meta expands Instagram's AI age-estimation tool worldwide
*AI & Tech · TechCrunch*

Meta announced on Nov. 9 that it is rolling out AI-powered age estimation on Instagram globally to identify teens who list an adult birthday and place them under Teen Account protections. The system analyzes profile context such as birthday mentions and school grades across posts, comments, bios and captions, and also checks visual cues in photos and videos. Teen Accounts are private by default, limit direct messages to people the user follows or is connected to, and hide harmful comments. The rollout continues on Facebook, with a similar Threads rollout planned later this year.
