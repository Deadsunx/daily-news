## 4. Hawley and Murphy introduce bipartisan AI Agent Accountability Act
*AI & Tech · Office of Sen. Josh Hawley*

Senators Josh Hawley (R-Mo.) and Chris Murphy (D-Conn.) announced legislation targeting cyberattacks carried out by AI agents against critical infrastructure such as hospitals, utilities and banks. Operators who knowingly run systems that recklessly cause hacking damage would face criminal and civil penalties under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act, and developers would be liable for failing to add adequate safeguards. The Attorney General and state attorneys general would be able to seek injunctions.

[Senate press release](https://www.hawley.senate.gov/senators-hawley-murphy-announce-bipartisan-ai-agent-accountability-act/)

## 5. ESA's Juice spacecraft completes Earth gravity assist toward Jupiter
*Science · ScienceDaily (ESA)*

The European Space Agency's Juice spacecraft flew past Earth on September 28 at 8,640 km above the Indian Ocean, changing direction by 20 degrees and gaining 3.5 km/s. The maneuver needed only one of six planned course corrections, preserving propellant, and the craft tested its 10 science instruments during the encounter. A third and final Earth flyby is planned for January 2029, with arrival at Jupiter in 2031.

[ScienceDaily report](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261001214102.htm)

## 6. Chang'e-6 lunar soil reveals previously unknown magnetic iron mineral
*Science · ScienceDaily*

Researchers at the Hefei Institutes of Physical Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, identified gamma-phase iron, a magnetic mineral not previously found in natural Moon samples, in impact-glass particles from Chang'e-6 soil. It was the dominant form of iron in the two impact-glass samples studied, per the work published in PNAS. The nanoscale particles can hold stable magnetic signals, so they may record the Moon's ancient magnetic field.
