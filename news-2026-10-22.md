# Daily News — 2026-10-22

## 1. Russia frees 74-year-old U.S. citizen Stephen Hubbard as Witkoff meets Putin
*World & Geopolitics · Democracy Now!*

Russia released Stephen Hubbard, a 74-year-old U.S. citizen imprisoned for more than four years on charges of fighting as a mercenary in Ukraine. U.S. envoy Steve Witkoff, who was meeting Vladimir Putin in St. Petersburg, thanked Putin for the release. It follows an energy deal between Donald Trump and Putin to import Russian diesel, and Trump's announcement waiving a sanctions law aimed at Russian oil and gas revenues. Democracy Now! also cited a Wall Street Journal report that Elon Musk downgraded bandwidth on a military-grade Starlink service used in Ukraine; that report was not independently checked.

## 2. France and Italy tighten security ahead of more cost-of-living protests
*World & Geopolitics · Democracy Now!*

French Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu announced a major police deployment before more nationwide protests this weekend. Hundreds of thousands marched in more than 140 French cities on Saturday over the cost of living and education, and about 2,400 protesters were arrested. In Italy, the government is sending 500 troops to Milan through the end of the year after days of protests over rising living costs. Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni also approved measures extending preventive detention to minors.

## 3. Houthi attack on Taiz kills two girls as Yemen displacement passes 250,000
*World & Geopolitics · Democracy Now!*

Ansar Allah (Houthi) fighters attacked a residential area of Taiz on Tuesday, killing two teenage girls and wounding six people. The group has nearly surrounded the government-held city and cut most supply routes, and UN human rights chief Volker Türk warned that food is running short. The death toll from an Ansar Allah missile strike on a government training camp in southern Yemen rose to 73. The UN Refugee Agency reports more than 250,000 people displaced since fighting resumed in July.

## 4. U.S. stocks rise as SpaceX results lift tech; Nasdaq up 1.2%
*Business & Markets · Yahoo Finance*

## 5. OpenAI acquires Orchestra Bio to expand life-sciences work
*AI & Tech · OpenAI*

OpenAI announced on October 20 that it is acquiring Orchestra Bio, a platform that helps biopharma teams coordinate drug development using people and AI agents. OpenAI plans to combine Orchestra's team and technology with Rosalind, its set of AI models and tools for life-sciences research. The announcement names Novo Nordisk and Moderna as partners and does not disclose the price or terms of the deal.
