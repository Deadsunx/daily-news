## 1. Xi greets Trump on the tarmac in Shenzhen ahead of first three-way meeting with Putin
*World & Geopolitics · Forbes*

President Trump landed at Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport on Wednesday for the APEC summit, where Chinese President Xi Jinping met him personally at Air Force One, a departure from the usual practice of having senior officials receive visiting leaders. The White House confirmed that Trump, Xi and Russian President Vladimir Putin will hold their first-ever trilateral meeting on Thursday, Nov. 19, on the summit's sidelines, alongside a separate Trump-Xi bilateral meeting and working lunch. U.S. officials said nuclear arms control and the ongoing Iran war will be key topics, and Trump has suggested he will raise Russia's war in Ukraine. The summit comes amid U.S.-China trade tensions, including new U.S. restrictions on China's access to advanced AI chips.

## 2. Saudi-led coalition strikes Houthi targets across Yemen as Iran fires drones at Kuwait air base
*World & Geopolitics · Associated Press (via KSAT)*

The Saudi-led coalition launched a major air campaign across Houthi-held Yemen beginning Tuesday, saying it destroyed 60 military targets in Sanaa, Amran, Saada and Hodeida; Houthi health officials said at least 17 people were killed, including 15 at a sponge factory in Hodeida. On Wednesday the Houthis fired two cruise missiles toward Riyadh, which Saudi air defenses intercepted. Iran's army said it targeted Kuwait's Ali Al Salem Air Base with drones, which Kuwait said it intercepted, and Bahrain said it repelled a number of Iranian attacks, in apparent retaliation for U.S. fire the previous day. Separately, ADNOC said one of its vessels was attacked near the Strait of Hormuz, leaving one crew member unaccounted for and 11 injured.

## 3. Nvidia revenue doubles to $115.8 billion in first report since Jensen Huang's exit
*Business & Markets · NVIDIA*

Nvidia reported third-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year earlier and 20% from the prior quarter, with data center revenue of $107.6 billion, up 110% year over year. GAAP diluted earnings were $2.70 per share. The company guided fourth-quarter revenue to $130.0 billion, plus or minus 3%, in a quarter that includes an extra week, and said the outlook assumes no data center compute revenue from China. Interim CEO and CFO Colette Kress said the Vera Rubin platform is "off to an explosive start" and the roadmap is on track.

## 6. Astronomers watch a star spiral into a giant companion in the Andromeda galaxy
*Science · Science News*

A study published in Nature on Nov. 18 reports what researchers describe as the first observation of a star being engulfed by its giant companion, in the Andromeda galaxy. The giant brightened for about seven years starting in 2018, more than doubled in size while keeping a nearly unchanged surface temperature, and then erupted in October 2025, shedding roughly a tenth of its outer gas. The team, led by Viraj Karambelkar of Columbia University, explains the event as a smaller star spiraling through the giant's outer envelope and likely merging with its core. The event is designated AT 2025abao, and the paper is titled "An inspiral of a star inside a common envelope with its giant companion."
