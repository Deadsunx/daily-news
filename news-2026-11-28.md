## 2. Lebanon's prime minister says a proposal is underway to keep UN peacekeepers on past year-end
*World & Geopolitics · Associated Press (via KRMG)*

Lebanese Prime Minister Nawaf Salam told the AP that France is preparing a Security Council resolution letting UNIFIL continue some functions, such as clearing mines, escorting humanitarian convoys and monitoring violations, during a drawdown after its mandate expires at the end of 2026. About 7,500 peacekeepers are in southern Lebanon and are expected to withdraw gradually over about six months once the mandate ends; US officials have been open to considering the arrangement. Salam said the plan would 'give us some extra time to agree on a new architecture in southern Lebanon.' Israeli forces occupy much of southern Lebanon after a war with Hezbollah began in March, and Salam said formal talks with Israel are on hold because of Israel's elections.

## 4. Brazilian markets rally as Lula picks Bradesco chief Luiz Carlos Trabuco as finance minister
*Business & Markets · Associated Press (via ABC News)*

President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva confirmed that veteran banker Luiz Carlos Trabuco, head of Bradesco, will lead Brazil's finance ministry in his fourth term, which begins January 5. ## 6. Research flights find electric charge in layer clouds is linked to larger droplets
*Science · Interesting Engineering*

University of Reading researchers, publishing in Geophysical Research Letters, combined weather-balloon data with measurements from the FAAM Airborne Laboratory research jet. During a January 2024 flight over Reading, the jet dived into and climbed out of cloud tops 16 times, measuring electric charge, reflectivity and droplet size. They found stratus and stratocumulus clouds tend to have water droplets roughly 10% larger where more charge builds up at their tops. The finding could help refine climate models, which still struggle to simulate cloud cover.
