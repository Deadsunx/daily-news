## 1. US Navy Blockade Redirects 59 Vessels as Strait of Hormuz Crisis Persists
*World & Geopolitics · Iran Focus*

US Central Command said that as of August 12 its forces enforcing the naval blockade against Iran had redirected 59 commercial vessels linked to the Iranian regime, disabled three, and boarded two for inspection. The blockade, resumed July 14 after Iran's Revolutionary Guard renewed attacks on shipping, has coincided with a halt in Iran's oil exports, according to tanker-tracking data. Strait of Hormuz crossings remain far below normal, with only 14 ships transiting on August 11 versus the 120-150 that passed daily before the closure. Iran's IRGC-run Persian Gulf Strait Authority says the strait stays closed until its conditions are met, while President Trump has called the US blockade a "steel wall."

## 2. Silver Lake in Talks to Take Workday Private in Multibillion-Dollar Buyout
*Business & Markets · Bloomberg*

Private equity firm Silver Lake is in talks to acquire HR and finance software maker Workday, in a deal that would rank among the largest software buyouts on record, Reuters reported August 13. Workday shares surged as much as 26% and were halted for volatility on the news, a sharp reversal for a stock that had fallen about 15% this year and more than 40% from its 2024 peak. Silver Lake could bring in additional investors, following the model of its roughly $55 billion take-private of Electronic Arts last year alongside Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund. No agreement is final and talks are ongoing.

## 3. Google Launches Gemini 3.7 Flash, Its Fastest Coding and Agent Model Yet
*AI & Tech · Reuters*

Google unveiled Gemini 3.7 Flash on August 13, calling it its most capable workhorse model yet for software engineering, knowledge work and autonomous agent tasks. Released just three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash, it posts gains on coding benchmarks, including a jump from 34.4% to 43.6% on FrontierCode 1.1 Main and from 49% to 65.3% on DeepSWE v1.1, and supports a 1-million-token context window. Introductory pricing is $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens through year-end, half the previous Flash cost, and it's available immediately via the Gemini API, AI Studio and Gemini Enterprise.

## 4. French Tax Authority Confirms Data Breach Affecting Hundreds of Thousands of Taxpayers
*AI & Tech · The Star (Reuters)*

France's Finance Ministry confirmed late on August 13 that a cyberattack breached the country's tax authority, the General Directorate of Public Finances (DGFiP), extracting personal data on individual and business taxpayers. A hacker using the alias "ZeroBytes" claimed to have obtained more than 2 million records and posted a sample of 678,438 lines containing tax IDs, names, birth dates, addresses and income data on a cybercrime forum. The ministry said the attacker used stolen credentials and a multi-factor-authentication bypass dating to late June; the exact number of affected taxpayers remains under investigation, and those affected will receive individual notices.

## 5. Large Study Links Estrogen-Only Hormone Therapy to Lower Dementia Risk
*Science · ScienceDaily*

A study of more than 21,000 women published August 12 in Neurology found that those who used estrogen-only hormone therapy later in life had 39% lower odds of a dementia diagnosis and 35% lower odds of Alzheimer's-related brain changes found at autopsy. Among participants, 1,953 had used hormone therapy, typically starting after age 70, versus 19,509 who had not. Researchers cautioned that the women studied used hormone therapy decades ago, under different timing and formulations than current practice, so the results may not directly apply to today's standards.
