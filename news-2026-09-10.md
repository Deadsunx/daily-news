## 1. US Destroys Five More Iranian Oil Tankers as Iran Strikes US Base in Jordan
*World & Geopolitics · Al Jazeera*

The US military struck and destroyed five Iranian oil tankers overnight after Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps twice targeted a US Navy warship in the Gulf of Oman, US Central Command said. Iran retaliated by firing roughly 20 ballistic missiles at the Al-Azraq base in Jordan, of which Jordanian air defenses intercepted 18; no casualties were reported. Iran said it separately attacked 10 ships near the Strait of Hormuz. The exchange marks a further escalation in the six-month US-Israel war on Iran, which has largely closed the Strait of Hormuz to shipping.

## 2. Haaretz: Netanyahu Was Warned of Hamas Attack 10 Days Before Oct. 7, Didn't Brief Security Chiefs
*World & Geopolitics · Haaretz / The Times of Israel*

Israeli newspaper Haaretz reported that UAE President Mohammed bin Zayed called Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu about 10 days before the October 7, 2023 attack to warn that Hamas leader Yahya Sinwar was planning a major offensive. Haaretz said Netanyahu responded calmly, assured bin Zayed that Israel was prepared, but did not brief then-Shin Bet chief Ronen Bar or IDF chief of staff Herzi Halevi on the call. Netanyahu's office called the report an "absolute lie," and Netanyahu said he would sue Haaretz. The report has renewed political scrutiny in Israel over the government's preparedness ahead of the attack.

## 3. Brent Crude Tops $100 a Barrel for First Time Since July as Iran-US Conflict Escalates
*Business & Markets · Al Jazeera (AFP, Reuters)*

Brent crude rose to $100.19 a barrel on Wednesday, its highest level since July 24, as fighting between the US and Iran intensified in the Gulf. The surge followed US strikes on five Iranian oil tankers and Iranian missile attacks on a US base in Jordan. Global stocks slipped, with the S&P 500, Dow and Nasdaq all posting small losses and European shares hitting one-week lows on fears of energy-driven inflation. Analysts said oil would likely need to reach around $150 a barrel to meaningfully curb demand, while rising crude and diesel prices add pressure on central banks including the ECB and Federal Reserve ahead of upcoming rate decisions.

## 5. US Agencies Name Six Chinese AI Firms in Advisory on Mass Model Distillation
*AI & Tech · CISA / Unite.AI*

The NSA, CISA and FBI issued a joint cybersecurity advisory on September 8 accusing DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Alibaba, MiniMax, StepFun and Z.AI of running industrial-scale campaigns since late 2024 to extract capabilities from US frontier AI models including Claude, GPT, Gemini and Grok. The advisory says the companies used fraudulent accounts, bulk subscriptions and proxy "transfer station" services to evade detection, and that DeepSeek's widely cited $5.6 million training cost for R1 excludes the cost of distilled data. The agencies recommend that US AI firms quietly degrade responses for accounts suspected of distillation rather than blocking them outright, and share threat intelligence across the industry. CISA Acting Director Nick Andersen urged companies to safeguard their platforms against the practice.

## 6. OpenAI's Navier-Stokes Proof Draws Credit Dispute With Rival Mathematicians
*AI & Tech · Axios / Fortune*

OpenAI said a system of roughly 10,000 coordinating AI agents produced, over about 88 hours, an analytical proof and a Lean-formalized proof that Navier-Stokes fluid equations can develop a singularity in finite time, addressing part of a Millennium Prize Problem; the company said it would not seek the $1 million prize. NYU mathematician Tristan Buckmaster and Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge said they had independently pursued the same narrow approach for nearly a year and reached a solution by August 22, and Buckmaster alleged an OpenAI researcher pressured him over publication credit and timing. OpenAI has said it cannot rule out that de-identified usage data helped improve its models but has not credited the two researchers. The dispute has intensified questions about attribution as AI systems are increasingly used in frontier mathematical research.
