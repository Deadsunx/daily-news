## 3. China Sets Target to Quadruple AI Computing Capacity by 2030
*AI & Tech · South China Morning Post*

China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology released a five-year plan on September 7 setting a target of 9,800 exaflops of intelligent computing capacity by 2030, up from 2,185 exaflops at the end of June 2026, a 177% year-over-year increase. The plan commits 3.8 trillion yuan (about $532 billion) in cumulative information-infrastructure investment through 2030 and calls for deploying computing clusters using 100,000 or more accelerator cards. China had already built 52 facilities each with more than 10,000 accelerator cards as of June.

## 4. Physicists Directly Observe Quantum Object Obeying Einstein's Gravity for First Time
*Science · ScienceDaily / University of Oxford*

An international team including Nobel laureate Sir Roger Penrose used a custom-built Quantum Galileo Interferometer to split an ultracold rubidium atom's quantum wave, holding one part in place while letting the other fall freely under gravity, then measured the resulting quantum phase difference. The measured phase matched the prediction from applying Einstein's equivalence principle to a quantum object, marking the first direct measurement of this effect. The study, led by Ben-Gurion University, the University of Ulm and the University of Oxford, was published September 2 in Science Advances.

## 5. Thieves Steal Renoir Paintings Worth Roughly $10 Million from French Riviera Museum
*Culture · Al Jazeera*

Two thieves broke into the Musée Renoir in Cagnes-sur-Mer, France, just before 6 a.m. on September 8 and took four paintings by Pierre-Auguste Renoir, abandoning one as they fled after being spotted on surveillance cameras. Mayor Bryan Masson said the museum alarm sounded at 5:48 a.m. and municipal police arrived five minutes later, but the two suspects escaped; he estimated the stolen works' value at about 9 million euros ($10.5 million). France's organized-crime unit is leading the investigation, and the theft follows last year's high-profile Louvre jewel heist in Paris.
