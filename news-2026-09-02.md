## 4. Pentagon adds ChatGPT and Grok to its GenAI.mil platform for 3 million personnel
*AI & Tech · TechCrunch*

The US Department of War (Defense) launched OpenAI's ChatGPT Mil and xAI/Starshield's Grok for Government on its GenAI.mil portal on Monday, joining Google Gemini for Government and extending access to more than 3 million military and civilian personnel. GenAI.mil, which launched in December 2025 with Gemini alone, has already drawn over 1.7 million unique users; ChatGPT Mil is accredited for Impact Level 5 and will handle unclassified administrative, logistics and policy work. OpenAI said data processed on the platform stays isolated within the government environment and is not used to train its commercial models.

## 5. Physicists report 'most compelling hint yet' of dark matter at LUX-ZEPLIN detector
*Science · ABC News*

An international team using the LUX-ZEPLIN (LZ) detector, buried nearly 1.5km underground in a former South Dakota gold mine, reported a single energy flash consistent with a WIMP (weakly interacting massive particle) colliding with a xenon nucleus. The event, recorded on June 16, 2023 during a 220-day run and presented at the 2026 TeV Particle Astrophysics conference, reached 2.6 sigma significance, meaning there is roughly a 0.5% chance it is explained by known background sources, still short of the 5-sigma threshold needed to claim discovery. Researchers, including physicists Theresa Fruth and Nicole Bell, called the result exciting but cautioned it is a single event that needs confirmation from further data collection, which will continue at LZ until at least 2028.
