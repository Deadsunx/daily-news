## 1. Parti Québécois wins most seats in Quebec election
*World & Geopolitics · Al Jazeera*

The secessionist Parti Québécois, led by Paul St-Pierre Plamondon, won the most seats in Quebec's provincial election, returning to power after winning only 3 seats in 2022. A majority requires 64 of the 127 National Assembly seats, and it was not yet clear in the report whether the party reached that threshold. The result ends eight years of Coalition Avenir Québec government. Plamondon has promised a third independence referendum during his first term but said he would wait until US President Donald Trump leaves office in January 2029; support for independence is about 30 percent.

## 2. UN Human Rights Council condemns attacks on civilians in Sudan, calls for ceasefire
*World & Geopolitics · Al Jazeera*

The 47-member UN Human Rights Council adopted a resolution on Sudan by a vote of 28 to 10, with 9 abstentions. It demands an immediate and complete ceasefire by all parties without preconditions, an independent monitoring mechanism and restoration of basic infrastructure. The conflict, which began in April 2023, has killed tens of thousands and displaced over 13 million people, and drone strikes killed more than 1,100 people in the first five months of 2026. Sudan's government urged member states to reject the measure.

## 3. US gasoline averages $4.36 a gallon as Trump blames refinery closures and Ukraine
*Business & Markets · Al Jazeera*

The US average gasoline price is $4.36 per gallon, up from $4.14 a month earlier and $2.98 on February 28, when the US and Israel first struck Iran. President Trump attributed high prices to Russian refineries being hit by Ukraine and to refinery closures in states such as California. Reported data show Strait of Hormuz exports down 97 percent since the war began, California refining capacity down 17 percent over the past year, and the US Strategic Petroleum Reserve at its lowest level since 1982. An AP-NORC poll found 65 percent of Americans blamed Trump's policies for higher prices.

## 4. Mistral unveils 1-trillion-parameter Mistral Large 4
*AI & Tech · TechCrunch*

French AI lab Mistral AI introduced Mistral Large 4, a multimodal model with 1 trillion parameters, trained on about 4,000 Nvidia GPUs. The model is available through a public guardrail endpoint, with open weights planned in about three weeks after safety testing; benchmark results were still pending at publication. Mistral says it is optimized for cybersecurity, finance and chip design and positions itself as a third way between closed US and open Chinese models. The company is backed by investors including ASML and Samsung, at a Series D valuation of €21 billion.

## 6. Astronomers detect first confirmed second-generation planet around a white dwarf
*Science · Space.com*

A study in Nature Astronomy reports a Jupiter-sized gas giant orbiting the white dwarf HS 0209+0832, about 790 light-years away, with an orbital period of roughly 4.4 days. NASA's TESS spacecraft found the planet through transit dips in the star's light. Researchers say it is the first confirmed second-generation planet around a white dwarf, formed from material the star shed as it died. The star's atmosphere shows zinc, copper and niobium at about 1,000 times solar levels, which supported the planet's origin.
