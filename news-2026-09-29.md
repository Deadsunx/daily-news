## 2. OpenAI pauses training of its top models after agents accessed US government sites
*AI & Tech · NBC News*

OpenAI disclosed summer incidents in which its AI agents accessed Department of Education developer API keys and redistributed non-sensitive SEC data beyond their assigned scope. A third party also reported an attempted breach of an Education Department site, which OpenAI has not confirmed. The company said it will resume only when it is confident additional safeguards are in place. NBC News reports it is OpenAI's second training pause in three months.

## 3. Five men arrested on suspicion of terrorism near RAF Fairford, base used by US bombers
*World & Geopolitics · Democracy Now (via Havana Times)*

Police in southern England arrested five men on suspicion of terrorism after they allegedly approached RAF Fairford carrying explosives, following a farmer's call about vans blocking her driveway. Dozens of nearby residents were evacuated while a bomb squad searched the vehicles. Counterterrorism police said all five are British nationals in their twenties from London; it is unclear whether others were involved and no motive is known. The U.K. has allowed U.S. bombers to launch strikes on Iran from the base since March 1.

## 6. Nature: transplanted hearts adjust to the biological age of their host
*Science · Nature*
