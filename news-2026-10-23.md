# Daily News — 2026-10-23

## 1. Magnitude 7 earthquake kills 16 in China's Tibet
*World & Geopolitics · Al Jazeera*

A magnitude 7 earthquake struck Tibet, China, on October 22, according to Chinese state news agency Xinhua as relayed by Al Jazeera. At least 16 people were killed and 32 others injured, based on local medics' reports at the time of publication. Casualty figures were still being reported and may change.

## 2. US-sanctioned International Criminal Court wins EU's Sakharov Prize
*World & Geopolitics · Al Jazeera*

The International Criminal Court has been awarded the Sakharov Prize, the European Union's top human rights award. The prize was announced on October 22, less than two weeks after US President Donald Trump announced sanctions against the court. The award is given annually for work in defence of human rights and freedom of thought.

[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/22/us-sanctioned-icc-wins-sakharov-prize-eus-top-human-rights-award)

## 3. Study classifies 96 percent of assessed coral reef systems as critically endangered
*Science · Al Jazeera*

A new classification by the world authority on threatened wildlife places almost all shallow-water coral reefs at risk of extinction. It categorises 96 percent of assessed coral reef systems as critically endangered. The finding was reported on October 22.

[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/22/almost-all-shallow-water-coral-reefs-now-face-extinction-risk-study-warns)

## 4. DeepSeek reportedly raises $15B days after US restrictions
*AI & Tech · TechCrunch*

Chinese AI company DeepSeek reportedly raised about $15 billion (roughly 100 billion yuan), one of the largest funding rounds in China, according to a Bloomberg report cited by TechCrunch. Chinese battery maker CATL and Tencent were named among potential investors. The report came two days after the US ordered federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek's AI technology over security concerns. DeepSeek did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/deepseek-reportedly-raises-15b-days-after-us-restrictions/)

## 5. Railway urges all users to revoke secret keys after cyberattack
*AI & Tech · TechCrunch*

Developer platform Railway said in a blog post on October 21 that attackers may have accessed encrypted environment variables and, for some, obtained the decrypted contents. Initial analysis suggests a subset of users was affected, but Railway told all users to revoke and rotate their API and cloud keys. The company paused customer deployments during its investigation and has not disclosed the root cause or number of affected accounts. It said the breach was likely part of a broader campaign targeting platforms to compromise AI inference providers.

[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/developer-platform-railway-urges-all-users-to-revoke-secret-keys-after-cyberattack/)

## 6. Fusion startup Helion raises $225M to develop power semiconductors
*Science · TechCrunch*

Helion received a three-year, $175 million US Commerce Department award under the CHIPS Act plus a separate $50 million private investment, bringing its total funding above $3.2 billion. The money will fund advanced semiconductors and power electronics, which CEO David Kirtley said could cut electricity losses (currently about 5% to 10%) in half, a potential benefit not yet demonstrated. Helion is racing to finish its first power plant, Orion, to supply Microsoft by 2028, though Kirtley said this R&D will not reach Orion in time.

[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/fusion-startup-helion-is-developing-new-semiconductors-that-could-double-output-of-its-power-plants/)
