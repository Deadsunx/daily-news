## 5. Baidu releases open-weight ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base model
*AI & Tech · Baidu ERNIE*

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base on November 5, a sparse Mixture-of-Experts model with 280 billion total parameters and 14 billion active per token, pre-trained from scratch on 33.5 trillion tokens. Training context was extended to 128K tokens, and fine-tuning and inference support up to 1 million tokens. The weights are the end-of-pre-training checkpoint with no post-training applied, available on Hugging Face in FP8 and BF16 formats under a custom license. The release adds to the pool of large open-weight base models available for fine-tuning.

[ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base release](https://ernie.baidu.com/release/ernie-5-5-flash-base/)

## 6. Rubin Observatory preview data reveals two previously unknown Milky Way companions
*Science · ScienceDaily (Yale University)*

Preview observations from the Vera C. Rubin Observatory, covering about 7% of the night sky, revealed Aquarius IV, an ultra-faint galaxy about 355,000 light-years away, and Rubin-GC1, a star cluster hidden by cosmic dust. The Aquarius IV finding appears in Research Notes of the AAS, and Rubin-GC1 has been accepted by The Astrophysical Journal Letters. About 65 Milky Way satellite galaxies are known, and some estimates suggest hundreds more remain undiscovered. The 10-year Legacy Survey of Space and Time began in June.

[Rubin Observatory Discovers Two Hidden Companions of the Milky Way](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/11/261104012252.htm)

## 7. NASA's Swift Observatory re-enters Earth's atmosphere after nearly 22 years
*Science · ScienceDaily (NASA)*

NASA's Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory, launched in 2004, re-entered the atmosphere over North Sumatra, Indonesia, at 7:39 a.m. EST on a Wednesday, as confirmed by the US Space Force. It operated for nearly 22 years, more than ten times its planned two-year mission. NASA expected most of the spacecraft to burn up, though some components may have survived. Swift was built to study gamma-ray bursts and later contributed to research on active galaxies, supernovae, and comets.

[NASA's Swift Telescope Falls to Earth After Nearly 22 Years in Space](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/11/261105224545.htm)
