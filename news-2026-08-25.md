## 1. US Treasury Unveils Sweeping New Sanctions on Iran in 'Operation Economic Outcast'
*World & Geopolitics · NPR*

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced a new round of U.S. sanctions on Iran on Monday, calling the campaign "Operation Economic Outcast" and describing it as part of what President Trump has termed an "economic D-Day." The measures target a network of brokers, companies, and shadow-fleet vessels operating across the UAE, Hong Kong, China, Singapore, Switzerland, and Europe that transport Iranian oil and channel revenue to the IRGC-Qods Force. Iran's security chief Mohsen Rezaei vowed a "seismic" retaliation and threatened to halt oil flow through the Strait of Hormuz. Iran's rial currency plunged past 2 million to the dollar in anticipation of the sanctions, with the country's inflation rate now near 90%.

## 2. Landfill Collapse in Guinea's Capital Kills at Least 30
*World & Geopolitics · NBC News*

A landslide at the Dar Es Salam landfill in Conakry, Guinea, killed at least 30 people after overnight heavy rain caused the waste mountain to collapse onto nearby homes early Sunday. Six critically injured survivors were taken to hospital, and several homes were destroyed as rescuers used excavators to search for more victims. The dump, the largest in the West African capital of roughly 2 million people, had been scheduled for closure the same day the collapse occurred; many residents facing eviction notices were still living nearby. Guinean Prime Minister Amadou Oury Bah visited the site as search-and-rescue operations continued.

## 4. XPeng's Robotics Unit Raises Over $900 Million at $6.3 Billion Valuation
*AI & Tech · Electrek*

Chinese EV maker XPeng announced Monday that its robotics business raised more than $900 million at a post-money valuation exceeding $6.3 billion, which the company calls the largest single-round private financing in the history of China's embodied AI industry. The round was led by IDG Capital, with participation from Gaorong Ventures and strategic backing from Tencent and Alibaba. The funding will support mass production of XPeng's IRON humanoid robot, which runs on three in-house Turing AI chips delivering a combined 2,250 TOPS and is targeted to enter production by the end of 2026. XPeng is spinning the unit into a standalone subsidiary over the next 18 months while retaining about 82% ownership.

## 5. NASA's Roman Space Telescope Cleared for Aug. 30 Launch
*Science · NASA / ScienceDaily*

NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope has been certified ready for final launch preparations after mission managers from NASA and SpaceX completed a Flight Readiness Review at Kennedy Space Center. The observatory is targeted to lift off no earlier than 7:26 a.m. EDT on Sunday, August 30, aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket from Launch Complex 39A. Roman is designed to study the universe's expansion and history and to survey large regions of the sky for exoplanets, giving astronomers a much wider field of view than previous space telescopes. Teams will next move the encapsulated observatory to SpaceX's hangar at pad 39A to connect it to the rocket ahead of transport to the launch pad.
